1. Text hierarchy

Body copy uses Open Sans at a comfortable reading size. Highlight key terminology, emphasize important ideas, or use a short highlighted phrase. Use inline_code() for Python names.

Secondary heading

Keep paragraphs short, focused, and easy to scan. Use consistent spacing across chapters.
